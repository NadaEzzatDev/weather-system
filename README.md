# 🌦️ Mini Weather System | Interactive Wiring Experience

### From sensing the environment to bringing data to life!

**What if students could change the weather and watch their Arduino respond in real time?**

The **Mini Weather System – Interactive Wiring Experience** turns an Arduino circuit into an interactive engineering adventure. Students explore the components, solve wiring challenges, and discover how temperature data travels from a sensor to a display.

Instead of memorizing connection diagrams, they build their understanding one step at a time — making predictions, answering questions, and watching the circuit come to life.

## ✨ Explore the Experience

- **🔍 Explore the Components** — Discover the role of each component and follow the data journey: **DHT11 → Arduino → LCD**.
- **🧠 Solve Wiring Challenges** — Answer questions at every stage and receive instant feedback to guide your progress.
- **⚡ Build the Circuit Step by Step** — Reveal each connection as you progress through the interactive experience.
- **📟 Bring the LCD to Life** — Watch the display power on and then show the weather readings.
- **🌡️ Create Different Weather Conditions** — Experiment with temperature and humidity sliders or try presets such as summer, classroom, and rainy day to see how the displayed readings change.
